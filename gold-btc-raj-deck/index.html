Summary

Where the work stands

  • Gold: a returns-space model from variables already in our CMAs. Monthly R² 0.31, quarterly 0.45; betas stable in rolling windows; beats a random walk out of sample. The data cannot set the intercept — that is the judgment call, and I propose a structural growth band plus a valuation overlay.
  • Bitcoin: twelve candidate drivers, none stable. No model is particularly consistent. The approach sizes BTC as a share of gold's monetary role with a survival branch — the unknown is named, not hidden.

Point 1 · Literature reviews

What the evidence establishes; what the street does with it

  • Real rates are the engine. Gold trades like a long-duration real bond. Highest single-factor R². The fit broke after 2022.
  • The dollar is the steadiest relationship — and it held through 2022.
  • Inflation passes through only at long horizons. The 1–20y hedge evidence is weak.
  • Official-sector demand broke the fixed-mean models. Price-insensitive, sanctions-driven, unforecastable as a factor.
  • Nothing beats a random walk on the level. VAR, GARCH, ML, stock-to-flow all fail out of sample.
The gap: the marginal buyer that moved the price — EM central banks — is modeled by almost nobody.

Point 2 · Key drivers

Three variables carry gold; nothing carries Bitcoin

Gold — survived testing:

  • Real 10y (TIPS) — the engine; β ≈ -8.292 %/pp monthly.
  • Dollar — one floating DM leg (slide 11 for which one).
  • Breakevens / inflation swaps — the forward inflation leg. Realized CPI adds nothing.
  • MOVE — rate-vol insurance bid, second-order.
  • High-CPI regime × real rate — the sensitivity is state-dependent; this is where uncontrolled inflation lives.

Bitcoin — the same exercise returns empty:

  • Equity/liquidity beta is the only supported link — risk appetite, cycle-scale, no decade drift.
  • Network adoption is the only defensible drift and it is reflexive (price attracts users).
  • Inflation hedge, stock-to-flow, on-chain valuation: not supported.
Consequence: gold gets a factor CMA. Bitcoin gets a share-of-gold framework with a survival branch (slides 14–16).

Point 4 · The error term (2/2)

Gold keeps pace with the asset pool only through ~20-year cycles — drift needs a valuation overlay

  • Levels-space statistics cannot rescue this: reversion half-lives of 6–70 years give ~1–4 independent episodes in 55 years. The tether cannot be estimated, only defended.
  • 150 years of data: the tracking is a post-1971 property. Under the peg, money grows past a fixed gold price; the gap releases in jumps (1933, 1971).
Treatment: intercept = growth band 4.8–6.7%/yr (anchor choice) + qualitative valuation overlay for the position in the share cycle. At the 97th percentile, the overlay argues for the low end.

  • Fed float-weighted major index encompasses DXY (t = −3.7 vs +1.0). DXY survives as the live daily stand-in.
  • Demand weights fail mechanically: CNY/INR carry the weight, contribute no return variance.
  • The twist: marginal buyer and average private holder are the same ~60% China+India object — best single measure since 2022 (R² .30) and the only significant addition in that window. 10y rolling test: not yet a trend (t −1.9; a 2000 spike decayed to zero).
Ask: one floating DM index (simple), or a regime-dependent leg that tilts to the buyer basket when official-sector flow dominates? This sets the beta we multiply against the firm's dollar path.
Same question, rate leg: is the US 10y real rate dollar-centric? If interest-rate parity held, one rate would suffice — but the marginal buyers' currencies are managed, so parity fails exactly where the demand is. Untested so far: a GDP-weighted global real rate vs the US real rate. Flagged as the next study.

Point 7 · Bitcoin's inconstancy (3/3)

The approach that owns it: a share of gold's pool, one named unknown, a survival branch

  • No factor CMA: with nothing stable, any regression number is a sample artifact.
  • The industry's own frame (CoinShares, ARK, VanEck) is share-of-a-monetary-pool. We make it honest: the share is the named assumption, not a model output.
  • Survival branch: a 20-year horizon carries real ruin probability (protocol, regulatory, displacement), partly offset by Lindy. The CMA is a mixture, not a point.
Ask: does a scenario grid on (share of gold's pool, survival probability) work as the CMA? It is the framing the evidence supports.
