Who owns the BTC ETFs? Institutional base composition

13F long shares by investor type, as a % of shares outstanding (period 2026-03-31). BTC ETFs vs equity-group means.

The BTC-ETF institutional base is thin and trading-desk heavy. In IBIT, hedge funds (magenta) nearly equal all long-only holders combined — the cash-and-carry basis trade. GBTC is the exception: almost all active long-only, ~0 hedge funds. Ordinary stocks are dominated by passive + active long-only.
Source: SEC Form 13F-HR bulk data set (period 2026-03-31); shares outstanding from SEC XBRL. Long-only = passive index + active long-only + pension + insurance + wealth/RIA. Vanguard co-filing entities de-duplicated; institutional totals can exceed 100% of float on high-short-interest stocks (securities-lending double-count). Curated investor-type classifier.
