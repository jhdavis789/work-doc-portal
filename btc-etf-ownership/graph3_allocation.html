What have long-only funds actually allocated to bitcoin?

Aggregate allocation of long-only managers to Bitcoin vs gold vs equity-index ETFs, as a % of their total 13F book (period 2026-03-31).

Real-money long-only investors have allocated almost nothing to bitcoin: ~0.02% of their aggregate 13F book vs ~0.13% to gold ETFs — roughly 7× more gold than bitcoin. Adoption is thin too: 23% of long-only funds hold any bitcoin ETF vs 47% for gold; the median bitcoin holder puts just 0.10% of its book there. Equity-index ETFs are larger because long-only funds use SPY/IVV/QQQ/IWM as cash-equitization building blocks. Caveat: 13F book = US-13F securities only, so per-fund weights for managers whose 13F is a small sliver of true AUM (e.g. sovereigns) are overstated — the aggregate figure is the robust one.
Source: SEC Form 13F-HR bulk data set (period 2026-03-31); shares outstanding from SEC XBRL (BTC/gold ETFs + stocks exact as-of; equity-index ETFs ≈ current, mature/stable). Long-only = passive index + active long-only + pension + insurance + wealth/RIA. Vanguard co-filing entities de-duplicated. By JAOD & (mainly) Claude.
