Long-only ownership — relative across asset classes

Long-only investors as a % of shares outstanding (13F, period 2026-03-31): Bitcoin vs gold vs equity-index ETFs vs ordinary stocks.

The relative gradient: Bitcoin ETFs ~10% long-only < Gold ETFs ~24% < Equity-index ETFs ~28% < ordinary stocks ~65%. Colors: Bitcoin ETF, Gold ETF, Equity-index ETF, S&P mid-cap, small-cap. Even buy-and-hold IVV (43%) far exceeds any bitcoin ETF; the most-traded equity vehicles (SPY/QQQ ~15-17%) sit closest to bitcoin.
Source: SEC Form 13F-HR bulk data set (period 2026-03-31); shares outstanding from SEC XBRL (BTC/gold ETFs + stocks exact as-of; equity-index ETFs ≈ current, mature/stable). Long-only = passive index + active long-only + pension + insurance + wealth/RIA. Vanguard co-filing entities de-duplicated. By JAOD & (mainly) Claude.
